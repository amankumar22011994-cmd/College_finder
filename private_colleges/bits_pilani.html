About BITS Pilani

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1964 by G.D. Birla. Consistently ranked in top 30 NIRF. One of India's most prestigious private universities — admission purely on merit via BITSAT. Known as the 'MIT of India' by many.

Research & Innovation

20+ research centers. Strong PhD program. Collaborates with NASA JPL, MIT, and Fraunhofer Institute. 500+ patents filed and active industry-funded research projects across all campuses.

Practice School

Unique Practice School (PS) program — mandatory industry internship at 200+ partner companies in India and abroad. Students work at Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, Texas Instruments, and NASA partners.

Global Collaborations

Dual degree programs with UIUC, Purdue, and Carnegie Mellon. Extensive alumni network in Silicon Valley. Strong connections with US universities for MS/PhD pathways.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

9 hostels — all residential campus. 100% accommodation guaranteed. Vibrant hostel culture with self-governance by student bodies.

🎭

OASIS

BITS Pilani's legendary cultural fest — one of the oldest and most celebrated in India. 4-day extravaganza with celebrity performances and 50,000+ visitors.

⚙️

APOGEE

Flagship technical fest and one of Asia's largest. 3-day event with robotics, hackathons, and talks by global tech innovators.

🏏

Sports

BITS has top-tier sports facilities — cricket, football, tennis, swimming pool, athletics track, squash courts, and a fully equipped gymnasium.

🌵

Pilani Location

Unique desert campus in Rajasthan. Peaceful, focused academic environment away from city distractions — perfect for deep learning.

🍽️

Dining

Multiple messes and food courts. BITSian food culture is legendary — Shiv Ganga, Connaught Place eateries within campus vicinity.

📚

Central Library

One of India's best private university libraries. Massive digital journal access including ACM, IEEE, Springer, and Elsevier databases.

🚀

Student Clubs

150+ clubs — CERN, SEDS, BITS-ACM, Finance Club, Drama Society, and multiple technical project groups with strong alumni mentorship.
