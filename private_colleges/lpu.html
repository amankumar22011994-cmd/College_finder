About LPU Punjab

Massive Scale

One of the largest single-campus universities in India. Hosts students from 50+ countries and all 28 Indian states, creating a global cultural melting pot.

Innovation Hub

Home to one of the biggest incubation centers in North India. Students have built solar-powered cars, buses, and numerous IoT-based agricultural tools.

Industry Immersion

Curriculum co-designed with companies like Google, SAP, and Microsoft. Over 100+ "Centres of Excellence" established by industry giants on campus.

Sports Excellence

LPU students won 13 medals at the Tokyo Olympics. The university provides world-class stadiums and indoor sports arenas for professional training.
