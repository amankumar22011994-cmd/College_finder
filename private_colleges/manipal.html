About Manipal Institute of Technology

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1957 — one of India's oldest private engineering colleges. Part of Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), a top-10 deemed university. Known for producing globally respected engineers across 6 decades.

Research & Innovation

Active research in robotics, marine engineering, and structural engineering. MIT Manipal has launched a CubeSat in collaboration with ISRO. Strong DST-funded research projects ongoing.

Unique Location

Located in Manipal — India's university town by the Arabian Sea coast. Serene coastal Karnataka environment. University campus is essentially a self-sufficient township with hospitals, courts, and shopping areas.

Global Collaborations

Active exchange programs with universities in USA, Germany, UK, and Australia. 100+ global MoUs. MIT Manipal alumni network is exceptionally active in Silicon Valley and the Gulf region.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

15+ hostels across the Manipal university campus. AC rooms, gym, sports facilities in each hostel block. Fully residential university township.

🎭

MIT Utsav

Annual cultural fest with music, dance, drama, and fashion. Manipal's unique coastal Karnataka culture adds a distinct flavour to campus celebrations.

⚙️

Techtatva

MIT Manipal's flagship technical fest — one of Karnataka's top college tech events with robotics, coding, and marine engineering challenges.

🌊

Coastal Location

Manipal is 8 km from Udupi and 60 km from Mangalore. Arabian Sea beaches at Malpe (8 km), Kapu, and Padubidri — weekend getaways from campus.

🍽️

Dining

20+ restaurants and canteens. End Food Court, International food stalls, and regional Karnataka and Kerala cuisine. Pizza, Chinese, and fast food options.

📚

Central Library

3 lakh+ books with full digital access. Marine engineering reference collection is one of India's best. Air-conditioned reading halls open till midnight.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, tennis, badminton, and a swimming pool. Annual MIT sports fest draws college teams from Karnataka and Goa.

🚀

Student Clubs

150+ clubs — MIT Robotics Club, ISTE, IEEE, Marine Society, Photography, Dramatics, Music, and an active MIT Manipal Entrepreneurship Cell.
