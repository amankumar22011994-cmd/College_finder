About BITS Goa

Legacy & Prestige

Established in 2004 as the second campus of BITS Pilani. Shares the same BITSAT admission process and academic standards as the mother campus. Consistently ranked among India's top private engineering colleges.

Research & Innovation

Active research in renewable energy, biotechnology, and computer science. Hosts DST-funded projects and collaborates with Goa's tourism and environment sectors for applied research.

Practice School

Same prestigious Practice School program as Pilani campus. Students intern at 200+ companies globally including Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, and leading MNCs across India and USA.

Goa Advantage

Unique coastal location in beautiful South Goa. Proximity to Vasco da Gama, Panaji, and Goa's growing IT and pharma sector. Unmatched quality of life on any Indian campus.
