About VIT Chennai

Legacy & Prestige

Established in 2010 as the Chennai campus of VIT University. Located in Vandalur — just 20 km from Chennai city. Benefits from VIT's strong brand, FFCS system, and placement network.

Chennai Advantage

Located near Chennai's IT corridor — Siruseri IT Park, Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR), and Tidel Park. Access to 200+ IT companies for internships and placements right at doorstep.

Research & Innovation

Active research in embedded systems, AI, and renewable energy. Tied to VIT's 20+ research centers. Chennai's pharma and auto industry provides unique applied research opportunities.

Industry Connect

Strong MoUs with Hyundai, Ford India, Amazon, and Cognizant. Regular industry guest lectures, live projects, and mentor programs through Chennai's massive corporate ecosystem.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

12 hostels with modern amenities. Separate accommodation for boys and girls. AC rooms, cafeteria, 24/7 Wi-Fi, and gym available.

🎭

Cultural Fest

Annual cultural fest with music, dance, drama and celebrity performances. Chennai's student community adds vibrant energy to VIT Chennai events.

⚙️

Tech Fest

Annual technical fest with hackathons, AI/ML competitions and IoT challenges leveraging Chennai's booming tech ecosystem.

🏙️

Chennai Location

Vandalur — 20 km from Chennai city. OMR IT corridor (1,000+ IT companies) 15 min away. Chennai airport 35 min drive. Metro connectivity available.

🍽️

Dining

Multiple messes and food courts. South Indian cuisine messes, North Indian options, and fast food joints on campus. Zomato delivery available.

📚

Central Library

Well-stocked library with digital journal access. AC reading zones open till midnight. Group study rooms and individual carrels available.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, tennis courts, and gymnasium. Annual VIT Chennai sports meet with all branches participating.

🚀

Student Clubs

100+ clubs including IEEE, ISTE, NSS, NCC, Toastmasters, and domain-specific technical chapters. Strong industry connect events organized year-round.
