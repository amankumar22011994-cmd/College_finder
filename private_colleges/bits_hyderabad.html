About BITS Hyderabad

Legacy & Prestige

Established in 2008 as the third BITS campus. Shares same admission standards and BITSAT process as Pilani. Located in Hyderabad — India's second-largest IT hub — giving students unmatched industry proximity.

Research & Innovation

Active research in AI, pharma tech, and materials science. Proximity to Hyderabad's pharma corridor gives students unique research opportunities in biotech and drug discovery.

Practice School

Same Practice School as Pilani. Students intern at companies in Hyderabad's IT hub — Infosys, Microsoft, Amazon, and pharma giants like Dr. Reddy's and Cipla.

Hyderabad Advantage

Located near HITECH City — Hyderabad's IT corridor with Amazon, Google, Microsoft, and 300+ IT companies within 30 minutes. Best location for tech internships among all BITS campuses.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

8 hostels on a fully residential campus. All students get on-campus accommodation. Vibrant hostel culture unique to all BITS campuses.

🎭

Pearl

BITS Hyderabad's flagship cultural fest. One of South India's most energetic college fests with celebrity performances and 40,000+ attendees.

⚙️

Atmos

Annual technical fest with cutting-edge competitions in AI, robotics, and IoT. Special pharma tech track unique to Hyderabad campus.

🏙️

HITECH City Proximity

5 km from HITECH City — Hyderabad's IT hub with Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and 300+ companies. Best internship access of any private campus in India.

🍽️

Dining

Multiple messes and food courts with diverse Indian cuisine. Special Hyderabadi biryani culture in mess and nearby dhabas.

📚

Central Library

Large digital library with IEEE, ACM, Elsevier access. Special pharma research database for biotech and chemistry students.

Sports
