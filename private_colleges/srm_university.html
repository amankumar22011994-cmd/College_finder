About SRM University

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1985 by Dr. T.R. Paarivendhar. One of India's largest private universities with 52,000+ students. Consistently in NIRF top 30. SRM has built a massive global alumni network across 40+ countries.

Research & Innovation

25+ research centers including TIFAC-CORE, SRM nanotechnology center, and medical research labs. Owns a satellite — 'SRMSAT' — launched by ISRO in 2011. 1,500+ publications annually.

Medical College Advantage

Unique among tech universities — SRM has its own medical college, dental college, and SRM Hospital on campus. Medical tech, pharma, and health informatics students get direct clinical exposure.

Global Collaborations

700+ MoUs with international universities. Campuses in Delhi-NCR, Andhra Pradesh, and Sikkim. Student exchange programs with 100+ global universities including Purdue and Coventry.

Campus Life

Hostels

40+ hostels with capacity for 20,000+ students. AC and non-AC options. International student hostels available. 24/7 Wi-Fi, gym, and dining.

🎭

Aarush

SRM's massive cultural fest — one of Tamil Nadu's biggest. 5-day event with international artists, fashion shows, and star performances drawing 75,000+ attendees.

⚙️

Invictus

Annual tech and management fest with AI, robotics, coding contests, and business case study competitions drawing national participation.

🏥

Medical Campus

Unique feature — SRM Hospital with 1,200+ beds, dental college, and pharmacy college all on campus. Biotech and health-tech students get clinical exposure.

Dining

50+ dining outlets including mess, food courts, Subway, Domino's, and South Indian eateries. One of India's largest campus food ecosystems.
