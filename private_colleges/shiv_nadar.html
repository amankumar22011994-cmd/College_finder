About SNU Greater Noida

Multidisciplinary Research

Recognized as an "Institution of Eminence." Promotes undergraduate research from year one through the OUR (Opportunities for Undergraduate Research) program.

STEAM+ Curriculum

Unique curriculum combining Science, Tech, Engineering, Arts, and Math. Students can major in Engineering while minoring in Economics or Design.

Industry Tie-ups

Strong partnership with HCL Technologies and other global firms. Features a dedicated Atal Incubation Centre to support student-led startups.

Lush Green Campus

A residential, biodiversity-rich 286-acre campus. Ranked among the youngest institutions in India to enter the NIRF Top 100 Overall list.
