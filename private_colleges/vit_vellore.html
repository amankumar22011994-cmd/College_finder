About VIT Vellore

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1984 by Dr. G. Viswanathan. India's largest private engineering university by enrollment. Consistently in NIRF top 15. Known for strong placement culture and massive alumni network across the globe.

Research & Innovation

20+ research centers including TIFAC-CORE, CBCMT, and Centre for Nanoscience. 2000+ research publications annually. Strong collaboration with NASA, Boeing, and Airbus for aerospace research.

FFCS System

Unique Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS) — students build their own timetable and can choose courses freely. No mandatory lectures — attendance is flexible, promoting self-directed learning.

Global Collaborations

600+ MoUs with global universities including Penn State, Georgia Tech, and KTH Sweden. 15% international students. Strong pathways to MS programs in USA, Canada, and Europe.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

30+ hostels with modern facilities. Separate blocks for boys, girls, and international students. AC rooms available. 24/7 Wi-Fi and cafeteria.

🎭

Riviera

VIT's international cultural fest — Asia's largest student-organized cultural fest. 5-day extravaganza with world-class artists, 1 lakh+ attendees.

⚙️

Gravitas

Asia's second-largest technical fest. Robotics, AI hackathons, and talks by global tech leaders. 40,000+ participants from 1,000+ colleges.

🏏

Sports

Olympic-standard stadium, international-level cricket ground, 5 football fields, 20 tennis courts, 3 swimming pools, athletics track.

🌆

Vellore Location

Vellore — Tamil Nadu's historic city. Excellent connectivity to Chennai (135 km). Famous Vellore Fort near campus. Peaceful academic town environment.

🍽️

Dining

40+ dining venues on campus including Domino's, Subway, and campus messes. VIT's food variety is famous among all Indian campuses.

📚

Central Library

4 lakh+ books. Digital access to 30,000+ journals. AC reading rooms. RFID-based smart library system. Open till midnight.
