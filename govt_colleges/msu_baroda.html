About MSU Baroda

Legacy & Prestige

Established in 1949 as part of the Maharaja Sayajirao University — one of Gujarat's most prestigious public universities. The Faculty of Technology & Engineering has produced engineers who lead Gujarat's chemical, pharma, and petrochemical industries.

Research & Innovation

Active research in chemical engineering, polymer science, and industrial automation. Collaboration with GSPC, ONGC, and Vadodara's petrochemical corridor. Multiple DST and GUJCOST funded research projects active.

Gujarat Industrial Corridor

Vadodara (Baroda) sits in Gujarat's golden corridor — between Ahmedabad and Surat. Proximity to ONGC, GAIL, Reliance Industries, and Gujarat's massive chemical & pharma belt creates outstanding internship and placement opportunities.

Industry Connect

Strong ties with Vadodara's chemical, pharma, and engineering industries. L&T's heavy engineering plant, GSFC, and Reliance Industries are all within 30 km. MSU-Baroda alumni lead Gujarat's core industries.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

On-campus hostel facilities for outstation students in Vadodara. MSU's large university campus offers accommodation in the heart of Baroda city.

🎭

Sparsh

Annual cultural fest showcasing Gujarat's rich folk arts — Garba, Dandiya, classical music, and modern events. Vadodara's artistic reputation adds unique energy.

⚙️

Tech Fest FTE

Annual technical fest with chemical engineering design challenges, industrial automation competitions, and coding events unique to MSU Baroda's industrial context.

🏙️

Vadodara Location

Vadodara — Gujarat's cultural and industrial capital. Between Ahmedabad (100 km) and Surat (130 km). L&T, ONGC, Reliance all within the city or nearby industrial belt.

🍽️

Dining

Mess and canteen on campus. Vadodara's rich Gujarati food culture — sev tameta, undhiyu, fafda, and jalebi — abundantly available in and around the university campus.

📚

Central Library

70-year-old library with strong chemical engineering and industrial engineering collections. MSU's inter-faculty library access gives engineering students access to arts and science books too.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, and kabaddi. Vadodara's love for cricket is evident — MSU campus grounds are active with matches throughout the year.

🚀

Student Clubs

Chemical Engineering Society, IEEE, Coding Club, Photography, Drama (Baroda's theatre culture), NSS, and the MSU Entrepreneurship Cell with Vadodara industry connects.
