About DTU Delhi

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1941 as Delhi Polytechnic, upgraded to Delhi College of Engineering (DCE) and became DTU — a full state university in 2009. One of Delhi's most prestigious engineering colleges with 80+ years of legacy.

Research & Innovation

Active research in EV technology, renewable energy, AI, and smart city solutions. Collaborates with Delhi government on urban planning and transport projects. 300+ publications and multiple patents annually.

Delhi Advantage

Located in North Delhi — Metro-connected campus. Access to the entire Delhi-NCR tech corridor. Proximity to government institutions, PSUs, and top MNCs in Noida and Gurugram. Best location value in govt colleges.

Global Collaborations

Active MoUs with TU Munich, University of Stuttgart, NTU Singapore, and IITs. Regular student exchange programs and joint research projects with German and Singaporean universities.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

8 hostels with modern facilities. Metro-connected campus in North Delhi. All outstation students get hostel accommodation.

🎭

Engifest

DTU's massive cultural fest — one of Delhi's biggest. Celebrity performances, competitions, and 50,000+ attendees from Delhi-NCR.

⚙️

Invictus

Annual technical fest with hackathons, robotics, and EV design challenges. Special smart city and clean energy tracks unique to DTU.

🏙️

Delhi Location

Rohini, North Delhi — Metro station right outside campus. Connaught Place 30 min, Gurugram 45 min, Noida 40 min — best connectivity for placements.

🍽️

Dining

Multiple messes and canteens. Delhi's legendary street food culture — momos, chaat, and rolls — available right outside campus gates.

📚

Central Library

Large central library with 2 lakh+ volumes. Digital journals, 24-hour reading rooms, and group study areas available for all students.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton courts, and a fully equipped gymnasium. Strong inter-college sports participation in DU leagues.

🚀

Student Clubs

100+ clubs — Coding Club, IEEE, SAE India (electric vehicle team), Photography, Drama, Music, and NSS with active project culture.
