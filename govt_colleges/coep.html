About COEP Pune

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1854 — one of the oldest engineering colleges in Asia, established by the British Raj. A Maharashtra government institution and recently converted to an autonomous technological university (COEP-TU). 170 years of engineering heritage.

Research & Innovation

Active research in automotive engineering, structural design, and robotics. COEP's Formula Student (Mindspark) racing car team is nationally famous. Strong collaboration with Pune's auto giants like Tata Motors and Bajaj.

Pune's Oldest Engineering Brand

COEP alumni lead Pune's engineering industry. Its brand value in Maharashtra is unmatched — alumni at L&T, Tata Motors, Bajaj Auto, and Cummins hold senior positions reflecting decades of trust in COEP graduates.

Industry Connect

Located in the heart of Pune's educational district — Shivajinagar. Access to Pune's 500+ manufacturing and IT companies. Auto, defence, IT, and startup sectors all recruit actively from COEP.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

On-campus hostel facilities in the heart of Pune. Shivajinagar location — access to FC Road, JM Road, and Pune's vibrant student life culture.

🎭

Mindspark

COEP's legendary annual national-level technical fest. Formula Student racing car reveal, robotics, and hackathons attract 30,000+ participants from India.

🏎️

Formula Student

COEP's team builds an actual race car for national Formula Student competition every year — one of India's top student engineering projects.

🏙️

Pune Location

Shivajinagar — center of Pune. FC Road, Deccan Gymkhana, and Koregaon Park all within 2 km. Best campus location in any govt college of Maharashtra.

🍽️

Dining

Canteens serving Maharashtrian cuisine. FC Road's famous misal pav, wada pav, and Pune's legendary food culture right outside campus gates.

📚

Central Library

Historic 170-year-old library with rare engineering texts. Modern digital access to IEEE, Springer, and JSTOR databases. AC reading rooms available.

🏏

Sports

Football, cricket, basketball, volleyball courts. COEP's 170-year sports tradition — multiple Maharashtra University sports champions in its history.

🚀

Student Clubs

80+ clubs — Formula Racing Club, IEEE, SAE India, Robotics, Photography, Drama, and COEP's historic Gymkhana student body with 170 years of tradition.
