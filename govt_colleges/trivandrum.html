About College of Engineering Trivandrum

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1939 — Kerala's oldest and most prestigious engineering college. Autonomous institution under Kerala Technological University. Consistently ranked among Kerala's top 2 engineering colleges with over 85 years of heritage.

Research & Innovation

Active research in embedded systems, VLSI, structural engineering, and green energy. Close collaboration with VSSC (ISRO Trivandrum), DRDO, and Kerala government's smart city projects.

ISRO/Space City Advantage

Trivandrum is India's space city — home to VSSC, LPSC, and ISRO's main launch support center. CET students have unique access to ISRO internships, projects, and career opportunities unavailable at any other college campus.

Kerala's Engineering Brand

CET alumni lead Kerala's engineering, IT, and Gulf-based industries. Strong alumni network in the Gulf countries — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar — and in the USA tech sector makes CET a globally connected institution.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

Well-maintained hostel facilities for all outstation students. CET hostels in Thiruvananthapuram — one of India's most livable cities. Separate facilities for boys and girls.

🎭

Cetus

CET's annual cultural fest showcasing Kerala's rich arts tradition — Kathakali, classical music, drama, and contemporary performances alongside modern celebrity shows.

⚙️

Tathva

Annual technical fest with electronics, robotics, and space tech competitions. Unique ISRO interaction sessions and live space technology demonstrations.

🚀

ISRO Campus Proximity

VSSC (Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre) is 15 km from CET campus. Unmatched access to India's space program — internships, project guidance, and career opportunities.

🍽️

Dining

Mess serving authentic Kerala cuisine — rice, sambar, fish curry, and payasam. Trivandrum's famous pazham pori (banana fritters) and chai culture near campus.

📚

Central Library

85-year-old library with strong technical collection. Rare books on early Indian space and engineering. Digital access to IEEE, Springer, and ASCE databases.

🏊

Sports

Swimming pool, cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, and badminton. CET teams regularly win in Kerala university games and South India competitions.

🚀

Student Clubs

60+ clubs — IEEE VSSC-supported Electronics Club, ISRO Science Club, NSS, Photography, Debate, and strong competitive coding and robotics groups.
