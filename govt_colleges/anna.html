About Anna University

Legacy & Prestige

Founded as College of Engineering Guindy in 1794 — one of the world's oldest engineering institutions. Became Anna University in 1978. Consistently NIRF top 10. Tamil Nadu's premier engineering institution with 230 years of legacy.

Research & Innovation

Exceptional research — 800+ publications annually. Active research in AI, automotive, aerospace, and biomedical engineering. Collaborates with ISRO, DRDO, and Tamil Nadu industrial corridor. Multiple DST-funded centers.

Chennai's Academic Crown

Located in Guindy — Chennai's industrial and academic hub. Guindy National Park adjacent to campus — rare eco-campus in a metro city. Close to Chennai's OMR IT corridor and automotive belt.

Global Collaborations

Active exchange with Georgia Tech, Purdue, IIT Madras, and AIST Japan. Anna University's affiliation governs 500+ engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu, making its research widely influential.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

12 hostels on campus. Guindy — one of Chennai's most well-connected areas. All outstation students get on-campus accommodation in clean, modern facilities.

🎭

Kurukshetra

CEG's iconic national-level technical fest. One of South India's oldest and biggest — 3-day event with 40,000+ participants, robotics, coding, and cultural events.

⚙️

Techofes

Anna University's annual technical symposium with inter-college competitions, guest lectures, and special aerospace engineering challenges unique to CEG.

🌿

Guindy National Park

Campus is adjacent to Guindy National Park — rare wildlife reserve inside Chennai city with deer, blackbucks, and peacocks. One of the most unique campus environments in India.

🍽️

Dining

Multiple messes serving authentic Tamil Nadu cuisine. Famous for filter coffee culture. Nearby Guindy's street food scene offers dosa, idli, and kothu parotta.

📚

Central Library

Historic 230-year library — one of India's oldest engineering libraries. Modern digital systems with full journal access. Rare engineering texts from British era preserved.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, athletics track, and swimming pool. Annual inter-department sports meet with 230-year sports tradition.

🚀

Student Clubs

100+ clubs — IEEE, SAE India (Aero), Robotics Club, ISTE, Photography, Drama, Music, and Tamil Nadu government collaboration projects for students.
