About Andhra University Engg

Legacy & Prestige

Founded in 1935 — one of Andhra Pradesh's oldest and most reputed engineering institutions. Part of Andhra University, a century-old state university in Visakhapatnam. 90 years of engineering heritage in Coastal Andhra.

Research & Innovation

Active research in marine engineering, ship design, ocean technology, and structural engineering. Unique collaboration with Eastern Naval Command, Visakhapatnam for defence and marine research projects.

Visakhapatnam Advantage

Located in Vizag — AP's largest city and a growing smart city. Major port, Steel Authority of India (SAIL), DRDO, and Eastern Naval Command all within the city. Core engineering students benefit enormously.

Industry Connect

Visakhapatnam's RINL Steel Plant, HPCL refinery, Eastern Naval Command, and Visakhapatnam Port provide live project, internship, and placement opportunities unique to AUCE students.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

Large hostel campus with accommodation for all outstation students. Visakhapatnam's pleasant coastal climate makes campus living comfortable year-round.

🎭

AU Utsav

Annual cultural fest celebrating Andhra's rich Telugu culture — Kuchipudi dance, music, drama, and modern events drawing students from across AP.

⚙️

TechFest AUCE

Annual technical fest with unique naval architecture and marine engineering challenges alongside IT, robotics, and coding competitions.

🌊

Vizag Coastal Location

Visakhapatnam — City of Destiny. Ramakrishna Beach, Rishikonda Beach, and Araku Valley nearby. Steel plant, port, and naval base within city limits.

🍽️

Dining

Multiple messes and canteens serving Andhra cuisine — spicy Andhra thali, pesarattu, and gongura dishes. Seafood culture of Vizag adds unique flavour to campus dining.

📚

Central Library

90-year-old library with strong naval architecture and marine engineering collection. Digital access to IEEE, ASCE, and marine engineering research databases.

🏊

Sports

Cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, swimming, and athletics. Annual AU sports meet with strong tradition across departments and hostels.

🚀

Student Clubs

Naval Architecture Society (unique to AUCE), IEEE, ISTE, Photography, Telugu Literary Club, NSS, and strong competitive programming and robotics groups.
