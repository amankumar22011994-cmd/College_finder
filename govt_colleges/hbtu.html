About HBTU Kanpur

Legacy & Prestige

Established in 1921 as Harcourt Butler Technological Institute — one of North India's oldest technical institutions. Upgraded to university status in 2016 as HBTU. 100+ year legacy in chemical, mechanical, and electrical engineering.

Research & Innovation

Strong research in chemical engineering, polymer science, and industrial engineering. Active collaboration with DRDO Kanpur and Kanpur's leather and chemical manufacturing industries. Multiple SERB-funded projects.

Kanpur's Tech Legacy

Kanpur was historically India's 'Manchester' — the industrial heartland. HBTU's graduates have led Kanpur's leather, chemical, and manufacturing industries for a century. Deep industry roots in UP's economy.

Industry Connect

Strong links with Kanpur's chemical, leather, and manufacturing sector. Proximity to IIT Kanpur allows academic collaborations. UP government IT projects provide additional placement avenues.

Campus Life
🏠

Hostels

Well-maintained hostel facilities for all students. Separate blocks for boys and girls. Mess, common rooms, and 24/7 security on the historical campus.

🎭

Techkriti-HBTU

Annual technical and cultural fest blending 100+ years of HBTU tradition with modern competitions and celebrity performances.

⚙️

Chemfest

Unique annual chemical engineering fest — one of North India's few events dedicated to chemical engineering competitions and industrial case studies.

🏙️

Kanpur Location

Kanpur — UP's industrial capital. IIT Kanpur 15 min away. Leather capital of India. Access to industrial plants for live project learning.

🍽️

Dining

Mess serving North Indian cuisine. Kanpur's famous street food — thathera brass, peda, and samosas — available near campus and in the city.

📚

Library

100-year-old library with rare chemical engineering and polymer science texts. Digital modernization underway with IEEE and journal database access.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, volleyball, and badminton facilities on the spacious 250-acre campus. Annual HBTU sports meet with deep inter-department rivalry.

🚀

Student Clubs

Coding Club, Chemical Engineering Society, Robotics, NSS, Photography, Drama, and the historic HBTU Alumni Association with strong Kanpur industry connections.
